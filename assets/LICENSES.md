## Original project assets

The geometry in `assets/environment/iron_crown/`,
`assets/environment/widowpine/`, `assets/environment/carrion_cut/`,
`assets/environment/vegetation/`, and `assets/environment/rocks/` is original
project work built by the four scripts in `tools/blender/`. Editable `.blend`
sources live in `art_source/`. The GLBs embed only the CC0 Poly Haven material
maps listed above; they contain no third-party meshes.

`assets/materials/original/widowpine_branch_rgba.png` is an original generated
RGBA foliage card made for this project with the Pine Tree 01 CC0 scans used as
botanical reference. The source scans remain listed above and the final card is
kept separate from them so its provenance is unambiguous.

`assets/materials/original/varkas_guard_fur.png` is an AI-generated guard-fur
base-color swatch created for this project on 2026-09-04. It is used on the
UV-unwrapped Varkas hero and his fur dressing; it is not a third-party scan
or part of the CC0 Quaternius source.

The wolverines of Varkas' warpack ride the Quaternius wolf rig, re-shaped by
bone scaling and dressed in the assets below. `assets/wolf/varkas_wolverine.glb`
is an animation-preserving, smooth-subdivided derivative of that same CC0 mesh,
generated by `tools/blender/build_varkas_wolverine.py`; Varkas' iron and bell
dressing is then added by `scripts/enemy.gd`.

## Warpack wolverine art (Higgsfield generations and original geometry)

Generated with Higgsfield (gpt_image_2_5), 2026-09, project use:
`assets/wolf/textures/fur_a.png`, `fur_b.png` (seamless wolverine-fur swatches,
projected by `assets/wolf/wolverine_fur.gdshader`), `iron_plate.png` (worn
riveted iron, used on all warpack armor and Varkas' plates) and
`warpaint.png` (a claw-mark mask projected as war paint and scars).

`assets/wolf/wolverine_head.glb` and its texture are the warpack's snarling
wolverine head in a spiked collar: a gpt_image_2_5 concept turned into a mesh
with Higgsfield (hunyuan3d_v3_image_to_3d), 2026-09, project use, then
decimated to about 28k triangles, faced down -Z and re-pivoted by
`tools/blender/build_varkas_pack_head.py`. The 33 MB raw generation is not
committed; the script documents how to regenerate the asset from it.

`assets/wolf/varkas_gear.glb` (rifle on a harness, pauldrons, collar, spine
plates, bell) is original procedural geometry authored for this game by
`tools/blender/build_varkas_gear.py`, editable at
`art_source/characters/varkas_gear.blend`; it uses no third-party artwork.

## assets/audio/ — Kenney Impact Sounds (CC0)
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds (Creative Commons Zero). Snow
footsteps and a handful of impact clips (`assets/audio/*.ogg`).

## assets/audio/generated/ — original sound design and score
Every other effect, ambience bed and music stem is original work rendered
offline by `tools/audio/render.py` (numpy synthesis with deterministic seeds,
no samples from any third party) into 44.1 kHz WAV/Ogg Vorbis. The generated
files, `scripts/audio_bank.gd` and the renderer are project work.

## assets/audio/voice/ — Higgsfield speech
Spoken lines (Herdkeeper narration, Varkas, warpack barks) were generated with
Higgsfield (`seed_audio`, preset voices Brooks, Vlad, Landon and Gideon), 2026-09,
project use. Takes are listed in `tools/audio/voice_manifest.json` and processed
(trim, level, EQ, saturation) by `tools/audio/voice_process.py`.

## Original Varkas facial sculpt

`wolf/varkas_head.glb` is original procedural geometry authored for this game:
a joined head, short tapered guard-fur ribbons, and a healed cheek scar.
The editable source is `art_source/characters/varkas_head.blend`, rebuilt by
`tools/blender/build_varkas_head.py`. It uses the existing original guard-fur
texture at runtime and adds no third-party artwork.

## Moon surface texture

`assets/environment/sky/moon_surface.png` was generated with Higgsfield (gpt_image_2_5), 2026-09, project use. It is sampled by `assets/materials/night_sky.gdshader` for the lunar disc; the clouds, stars, aurora and halo in that shader are procedural.
## Generated surface textures and props (Higgsfield, 2026-09)

Generated with Higgsfield (`gpt_image_2_5`), 2026-09, project use, then made
seamless and given normal/roughness maps by `tools/build_surface_textures.gd`:
`assets/materials/generated/` holds `frost_slate`, `carrion_sandstone`,
`iron_plate`, `abbey_masonry`, `banner_cloth`, `cracked_ice`, `dirty_snow`,
`iron_bound_timber` and `bell_bronze` (each `_albedo`, `_normal`, `_rough`).

`assets/environment/props/` holds image-to-3D hero props: concept images
generated with Higgsfield (`gpt_image_2_5`), 2026-09, project use, converted to
GLB with Higgsfield (Tripo H3.1 image-to-3D), 2026-09, project use:
`kill_stakes`, `iron_cage`, `war_banner`, `neck_bell`, `ammo_pouch`,
`bellthorn_tree`, `bellthorn_shrub`, `siege_wreck`.

`assets/materials/decals/` (footprints, paws, blood, scorch, dirty snow, ice
sheen) is original procedural work written by `tools/build_decals.gd`.
## assets/weapon/ — the Herdkeeper viewmodel

Generated with Higgsfield, 2026-09, project use. The reference art
`art_source/weapon/carbine_ref_*.png` and `glove_ref_*.png` came from
`gpt_image_2_5`. `assets/weapon/herdkeeper.glb` (Maren's carbine, with its bolt
handle, bell charm and a Blender-modelled box magazine split out as separate
parts) is `meshy_v7_image_to_3d` with PBR from `carbine_ref_b.png`;
`assets/weapon/glove.glb` (the gloved forearm, mirrored for the left hand) is
`hunyuan3d_v3_image_to_3d` from `glove_ref_b.png`. Both raw meshes were cleaned,
re-oriented, scaled and split by `tools/blender/build_herdkeeper_viewmodel.py`.
`assets/weapon/fx/` (muzzle flash star, smoke puff, spark dot, bullet-hole decals)
is original procedural art drawn by `tools/generate_fx_textures.gd`.
