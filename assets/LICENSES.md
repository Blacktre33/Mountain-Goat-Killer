and (for Varkas) plated in iron by `scripts/enemy.gd`.

## assets/audio/ — Kenney Impact Sounds (CC0)
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds (Creative Commons Zero). Snow
footsteps and a handful of impact clips. Every other sound in the game is
synthesised at startup by `scripts/audio.gd`.
